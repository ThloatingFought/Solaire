About Solaire

Solaire of Astora is a wandering knight from the distant land of Astora, journeying through the fading world of Lordran in search of his very own sun.
Clad in weathered armor and bearing the radiant symbol of his faith, he walks willingly into a world consumed by darkness, greeting its dangers not with despair, but with an unwavering sense of wonder.
